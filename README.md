Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

About the Project

This project focuses on understanding customer shopping behaviour through data analysis. The goal is to explore customer preferences, purchasing patterns, spending habits, and factors that influence buying decisions.

I used Python, PostgreSQL, and Power BI to clean, analyze, and visualize the data and turn it into useful business insights.

Tools & Technologies

- Python:Data cleaning and preprocessing using Pandas.
- Jupyter Notebook:Data exploration and analysis.
- PostgreSQL:Data storage and SQL-based analysis.
- Power BI:Interactive dashboard and data visualization.

Project Workflow

1. Data Cleaning & Preparation (Python)
- Loaded and explored the customer shopping dataset.
- Checked and handled missing values.
- Cleaned and standardized column names.
- Created customer age groups.
- Converted purchase frequency into days.
- Prepared the cleaned data for database analysis.

2. Data Analysis (PostgreSQL)
Used SQL queries to explore:
- Revenue generated by male and female customers.
- Customer spending with discounts.
- Top-rated products.
- Spending patterns based on subscription status.
- Products frequently purchased with discounts.
- New, returning, and loyal customer segments.
- Top-selling products across categories.
- Repeat purchases and subscriptions.
- Revenue generated by different age groups.

3. Dashboard (Power BI)

Created an interactive dashboard to present customer shopping patterns and business insights in an easy-to-understand visual format.
